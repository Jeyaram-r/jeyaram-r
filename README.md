# About Me

I am a **Software Engineer and Odoo Developer** focused on building reliable, scalable, and maintainable software systems. My engineering interests span **full-stack development, AI/ML, backend systems, cloud infrastructure, enterprise applications, and product engineering**.

I enjoy translating business requirements into production-ready systems with a strong emphasis on **architecture, clean code, security, performance, automation, and developer experience**.

My current engineering work includes designing and extending **Odoo-based enterprise workflows**, implementing business logic and access-control systems, working with **PostgreSQL and Docker**, and developing custom modules for complex business requirements.

I also have hands-on experience building AI/ML systems using **Python, Random Forest, K-Means, Pandas, Flask, and data-driven recommendation pipelines**, alongside modern frontend technologies such as **React.js, Next.js, and React Native**.

### Engineering Mindset

* Build systems that are **simple, scalable, secure, and observable**
* Prefer **clean architecture and maintainable abstractions**
* Understand the **business problem before implementing the technical solution**
* Treat performance and security as **engineering requirements**
* Automate repetitive workflows wherever possible
* Continuously improve through experimentation, debugging, and learning

### Open To

**Software Engineering · Full-Stack Development · AI/ML Engineering · Backend Engineering · Odoo Development · Cloud & DevOps · Product Engineering · Open Source**
